PB Score Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

PB Score is an Apple Watch pickleball scorekeeping application developed by Michael Villafana.

Data Collection

PB Score does not collect, transmit, or share personal information.

Data Storage

Scorekeeping information remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to external servers.

Third-Party Services

PB Score does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer support contact listed on the App Store.
